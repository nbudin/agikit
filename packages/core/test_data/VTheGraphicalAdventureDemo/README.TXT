Hi and welcome to V.

************************************************************************************************************************

How to play:
--> Move your player character with the arrow-keys on your keyboard or the mouse. Pressing a key once will make your character walk into this direction, pressing it agan will make him stop.

--> Tell your character what to do by typing in commands like for example when you're standing next to a tree:

    >look at tree
    You will recieve more information about the tree. When you want to take something you see lying around, simply do so by typing:

    >take silver key

    You can also interact with other persons, for example:
    >give hammer to the old man

    >ask woman about trains

--> Here are some verbs, the game will understand:
    LOOK       OPEN       PULL
    TAKE       CLOSE      PUSH
    GIVE       TALK       MOVE
    SEARCH     ASK        SHOOT
    TOUCH      SMELL      USE
    ATTACK     READ       DESTROY

--> By pressing the [Esc]-Key you can open the main menu.
    Here you can save and restore games, set the speed, toggle sound and joystick on and off or simply quit the game if you're really not man enough to beat the Visitors ;-)

--> Here are some shortcut keys:
    F1    - Help
    F2    - Turn on or off sound
    F3    - Repeat last line typed
    F4    - Look at inventory item
    F5    - Save
    F7    - Restore
    F8    - Travel
    F9    - Restart
    Alt-Z - Quit
    Tab   - Show inventory
